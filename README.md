# TSC-Tourney-Rules
The official Killing Floor Team Survival Competition Tournament rule set.

## Important Dates
- **TSC3** Sign-up begins: **Saturday, October 24, 2026, 17:00 UTC.**
- **TSC3** The first game: **Saturday, November 28, 2026.**
- **TSC3** Next games: every **Saturday** until **December 19, 2026**
- **TSC4**: **April - May, 2027**

## TSC3 in short — what your team needs to know to sign up

- **Free to enter.** There is no entrance fee and no deposit.
- **Team of 4, plus an optional 5th Reserve Player.** Everyone must own the original Killing Floor. The Captain must join the [Steam Group](https://steamcommunity.com/groups/TSC-Tourney) and [Discord](https://discord.com/invite/Y3W5crSXA5).
- **You can change your roster freely until the Tournament starts.**
- **Choose your Home Region: EU, NA, SA, or Mixed.** Players from other regions may join, but high ping is never an excuse.
- **Choose two Home Maps from the 21-map list.** A map taken by another team is no longer available, so earlier sign-ups get the wider choice.
- **Format: double elimination, 4v4.** Each game is two maps, one Home Map of each team, with a short Tiebreaker map if the score is tied. Every team plays at least two games.
- **The game: Killing Floor Team Survival Competition on the ScrN Balance.** Hell on Earth, perk level 6, 5 waves plus Overtime and Sudden Death per map. All perks are available with no per-perk limits; no custom weapons, perks, or zeds.
- **Servers: Duck Armada EU and US, 100 tickrate.** Which server a map is played on depends on both teams' regions.
