## 1. General
1. This *Tournament* is meant to be fun for everyone involved, so please encourage good behavior and fair play.
2. Not knowing the *Tournament Rules* is never a valid excuse for not following them.
3. References to any gender in any *Tournament Rules* do not imply these rules are gender-specific.
4. The **Tournament Administration** (**TA**) reserves the right to refuse entry to any team or player for whatever reason they see fit.
5. The *TA* reserves the right to change any rule if they find it unfair, exploitable, and/or imperfect.

## 4. Sign Up and Team Composition
1. Each team must consist of 4 players.
2. Each team member must have the original **Killing Floor** game in their *Steam* account.
3. Each team's **Captain** must join the [SteamGroup] and [Discord]. Other team members are encouraged to join as well.
4. Additionally, a team may sign up a 5th member as a **Reserve Player** to substitute for a team member if the latter cannot attend a tournament game.
5. A *Reserve Player* receives the same prizes as the other team members even if the former hasn't played a single game.
6. A team may replace any of its members **before** the *Tournament* has begun.
7. During the *Tournament*, a team may replace only the *Reserve Player* if the latter hasn't played a single game.
8. During the *Tournament*, a team may invite an additional *Reserve Player* if all its members (including the *Reserve Player*) have played at least one game. For example, team members P1, P2, P3, and P4 play the first game; P4 can't attend the second game and is substituted by P5; the team can sign up P6 as a *Reserve Player* for the third game. The rule `4.5` applies to both *Reserve Players* in this case.
9. Players cannot switch teams.
10. The only exception to the above rule is a *Reserve Player* who hasn't played a single game and has received written permission from the former team's *Captain* to join another team.
11. All games are played in **4v4** format except as per `10.2.3`.

## 7. Regions
1. Each team must specify its **Home Region** during the sign-up process:
   - **EU** - **Europe**.
   - **NA** - **North America**.
   - **SA** - **South America**.
   - **Mixed** - The team consists of players from both sides of the Atlantic Ocean (EU + NA/SA).
2. Other regions are not officially supported due to high **PING** (latency) to the *Tournament Game Servers*.
3. Players from other regions are allowed to join the *Tournament* if they can handle high *PING*.
4. A bad *PING* is not an excuse for losing the game or asking for any advantage or compensation.
5. A player with a very high *PING* (**300+**) can be kicked from the game if the *Referee* considers their "rubber-banding" game-breaking.
6. Players with an average *PING* above **100** on **all** *Tournament Game Servers* are not considered for team region selection. For example, 3 NA players + 1 Asian player => NA team.
7. *Reserve Players* do not affect the team's region until they actually play a *Tournament* game. For example, 4 NA players + 1 EU *Reserve Player* may be considered an NA team while only those 4 NA members are playing. Once the EU member joins the game, the team is reclassified as **Mixed**.
8. A non-**Mixed** team cannot get a "last-minute" substitute player from a different region.
9. A team can voluntarily claim itself **Mixed** to avoid the above limitation.
10. A team of `NA + SA` players has the **NA** *Home Region*.

## 9. Game Schedule

### 9.1. Team Pair Selection
1. The *TA* announces team pairs by posting game info on the [SteamGroup] forums and [Discord].
2. Each pair consists of two teams: **Red** (**British**) and **Blue** (**Steampunk**), where *Red* is the team with the higher *Rank* in the *Tournament Table*.
3. In the *Preliminary Round*, team pairs and their *Red*/*Blue* sides are chosen randomly.
4. Both team *Captains* can agree on changing the scheduled date/time if the *TA* approves it.

### 9.2. Game Server Selection
1. If both teams are from the same region, they play on their region's server. **SA** teams play on the **NA** server.
2. In an **NA vs. EU** game, each of the two maps is set on a different server, where a team plays its *Home Map* on the opponent's *Home Region Server*: *NA Home Map* is played on *EU Server* while *EU Home Map* is played on *NA Server*.
3. **NA vs. SA** games are played on the **NA** server.
4. **SA vs. EU** games get special treatment, as most SA-EU traffic goes via the USA's East Coast, making the **NA** server the perfect middle ground (compromise) for these games, where all players have **~100** *PING*.
5. The first map of an **SA vs. EU** game is always played on the **NA** server.
6. If, during the first map, 3 members of the **SA** team had an average *PING* **80+** each and the average *PING* of all 4 members combined was also **80+** (e.g., `68-84-92-100`), the second map is also played on the **NA** server. Otherwise (e.g., `68-72-92-120` or `40-84-92-100`), the **SA** team is treated as an **NA** team, and the second map is played on the **EU** server according to rule `9.2.2`.
7. The above rule applies only if the *EU Home Map* was played first.
8. The *Tiebreaker* map is played on the same server as the last map.
9. **Mixed**-region teams always play on their opponent's server.
10. The server for a **Mixed vs. Mixed** game is selected by the *TA*.
11. Both teams can agree to play on a specific server regardless of the region.

### 9.3. Map and Order Selection
1. The team *Captain* chooses one of the opponent's *Home Maps* to play.
2. The *Red Team Captain* chooses the map play order, except for the *Preliminary Round*.
3. The *TA* announces the **deadline** for the map/order selection.
4. If the team *Captain* hasn't posted their map choice before the *deadline*, the least recently played *Home Map* gets automatically selected (or chosen by the *TA* in the *Preliminary Round*).
5. In the *Preliminary Round* or if the *Red Team Captain* hasn't posted their map order choice before the *deadline*, the *Red Home Map* is played first, except for **SA vs. EU**, where the *EU Home Map on the NA Server* is played first to match the `9.2.4 - 9.2.7` rules.
6. **Game #12** forces the rules `9.3.4` and `9.3.5`, as the *deadline* expires before the team pair is known.

## 10. Game Progress

### 10.1. Pre-Game
1. Both teams should join the server **10 minutes** before their scheduled game time.
2. We removed Warmup because restarting a map after Warmup can risk a game crash. For example, a player could be fine during the warmup but crash after the map restart.
3. All players are encouraged to join the Tournament Server a day before a scheduled game to pre-cache the custom content and ensure everything is fine.

### 10.2. Game Start
1. The *Tournament Game* starts **exactly** at the scheduled time unless delayed by the *TA*, for example, if the previous game runs over.
2. The team *Captain* has the right to delay the start of the game for up to **10 minutes** due to technical difficulties. In that case, the team loses its pause-voting (`10.5`) and map-restarting (`10.6`) rights.
3. In an emergency, a team may start a game with **3 players** (in **3v4** format) if they cannot find a 4th member.
4. In a **3v4** game, TSC automatically balances team health by granting **133hp** and **+33% Syringe** healing rate to the smaller team members. Otherwise, teams are treated equally, and being the smaller team doesn't excuse losing the game or asking for any advantage or compensation.
5. The 4th player may join in later waves if they couldn't make it for the start.
6. If a team doesn't have even **3 players** for the game start, it gets **disqualified** from the *Tournament*, loses its place in the *Tournament Table*, doesn't get any *Prizes*, and loses its *Deposit*.
7. If a team starts a game with only **3 players**, all its other members who didn't participate in the *Tournament Games* get **disqualified** from the *Tournament* and don't get *Prizes*. The `4.5` rule no longer applies to the team.

### 10.3. Second Map
1. The second map is played in **Short** mode (5 *Main Waves*, no *Overtime*) if one of the teams scored 4 *Tourney Points* in the first map (`4:0`, *Main Time* win).
2. The second map is scheduled **5 minutes** after the first one ends if both are played on the same server.
3. The second map is scheduled **10 minutes** after the first one ends if it needs to be played on a different server.
4. A team cannot initiate the map restart (`10.6`) if it triggered the map restart or used **all** its pause votes in the previous map.

### 10.4. Tiebreaker
1. *Tiebreaker*, if needed, is scheduled **5 minutes** after the second map and is played on the same server as the second map.
2. *Game Pause* (`10.5`) and *Map Restart* (`10.6`) are prohibited in *Tiebreaker*.

### 10.5. Game Pause
1. Each team *Captain* has the right to initiate a **1-minute pause** (`MVOTE PAUSE 60`) **twice** in the game (once per map; twice on the same map and none on the other; or even twice in a row) or ask a *Referee* to force it at the end of the current wave.
2. Pauses can be called only at the start of *Trader Time*, during its first 20 seconds.
3. Pauses during a wave are prohibited.

### 10.6. Map Restart
1. If a team encounters technical issues at the map start (player crash, disconnect, visual glitches, etc.), the *Captain* may ask for a **map restart** (`MVOTE MAP RESTART` or ask the *Referee* to do so).
2. A map restart must be initiated within the first **30 gameplay seconds** of the match. The *Captain* can quickly chat "re" first (to meet the 30-second mark), then explain the case in the follow-up message.
3. The team can initiate a map restart only once per *Game*.
4. The team that initiated a map restart loses its pause-voting rights (`10.5`).
5. After the first **30 gameplay seconds** of the match, no excuses are taken into account (such as disconnecting, crashing, high *PING*, etc.). If you crash, reconnect as quickly as possible. If your team got wiped because of it, that's on you.

### 10.7. Technical Issues
1. Crashed/disconnected players may rejoin the game mid-wave, unless their team got wiped out during the reconnect.
2. Mid-wave rejoins are prohibited during a *Sudden Death* wave.
3. To keep a fair attitude but prevent cheating in a *Sudden Death* wave, a crashed/disconnected player counts as dead (wiping the team) if their health was below **80hp** at the moment they left the server.

### 10.8. Boring Vote
1. Players are allowed to initiate a *Boring Vote* (`MVOTE BORING`) to boost the zed spawn rate if they feel the game pace is too slow.
2. If the *Boring Vote* fails, the initiating team is prohibited from repeating the vote until the end of the wave.
3. Dead players are not allowed to initiate a *Boring Vote*.
4. A *Boring Vote* is prohibited in a **3v4** game.

### 10.9. Perk Selection
1. Any player can select any available perk.
2. There are no player-per-perk limits. For instance, the team can consist of 4 Berserkers.
3. Players may change perks every wave.

### 10.10. Weapon Combos
1. **Weapon Combos** that allow partially skipping the weapon fire or reload animation by switching to another weapon are **legal**.
2. The **Syringe** instant self-heal alias is **legal**.
3. Any commands, aliases, or macros that allow the player to bypass the weapon's fire rate and achieve DPS higher than intended are considered a **game-breaking** *Exploit*.

### 10.11. Door Welding
1. TSC automatically detects **10+ zeds behind a welded door** and **marks** the door for all players.
2. The *marked* door gets auto-destroyed in **20 seconds** (**10 seconds** in *Overtime*) if it is still under zed attack.
3. The door auto-destruct timer gets reset 30 seconds after the last zed attack.
4. **Total welding** inside a room or an area, where **all** doors are welded, and zeds are unable to reach the team, is considered an *Exploit* unless there are two or more active hidden zed spawn volumes (`ZombieVolume`) that cannot be blocked by player proximity or visibility.
5. The above rule is not applied during *Trader Time* or when an opponent player is inside the welded area.

### 10.12. Exploits, Glitches, and Tricks
1. Using any **Exploit** in the game leads to a warning (`MVOTE BLAME`).
2. If the player continues using the *Exploit* after the warning, they get forcibly killed by a *Referee*. However, they can return to the game in the next wave.
3. If the previous rule wasn't enough and the player continues using the *Exploit* after returning to the game, then they get kicked and banned from the game. Additionally, the *TA* may decide to **disqualify** this player or the entire team from the *Tournament*.
4. If the use of an *Exploit* leads to **game-breaking** consequences, a *Referee* or the *TA* has the right to make the following decisions:
   - a) **stop** the game, giving both teams points for waves survived before the issue happened;
   - b) force the game's **replay**;
   - c) **disqualify** the exploiter or the entire team from the *Tournament*;
   - d) revoke the *Deposit*.
5. Player `SUICIDE` is legal.
6. Players are not allowed to stay in a place **unreachable** by zeds (**Glitch Spot**). If a player stays there, it is considered an *Exploit*.
7. Switching to **spectator** mode during a game is considered an *Exploit* and may have game-breaking consequences.
8. Using **third-party tools** (**hacks**) that give players an unfair advantage (aimbots, wallhacks, crosshairs, etc.) during the game is strictly prohibited and leads to **disqualification**.
9. Every player is encouraged to record their entire *Tournament* gameplay to prove their gameplay is legit.

## 11. Refereeing and Player Conduct
1. All decisions made by *Referees* during a game are final and unchangeable, unless the game is under investigation by the *TA*.
2. If the problem is too complex and cannot be solved by a *Referee* during the game, it will be investigated by the *TA* after the match. The *Referee* informs teams and viewers when such a problem occurs.
3. Any complaints regarding a *Tournament Game* must be posted on the [SteamGroup] or [Discord] during the game or within **1 hour** after its end. If no complaints are received within that time, the game is officially closed, and no further investigation will be conducted.
4. The human factor must be taken into account in refereeing. *Referees* may forget to force `MVOTE PAUSE` or similar. *Referees* may not detect *Exploits* or other rule violations. Players or livestream viewers are advised to inform *Referees* or the *TA* as soon as possible when such cases occur. That can be done by posting on the [SteamGroup], in the [Discord], and/or in the official *Livestream Chat*.
5. Personal attacks, insults, or antagonism toward any *Tournament* members, *Referees*, or the *TA* via in-game chat, [SteamGroup] discussions, or comments will not be tolerated and will lead to **disqualification**.
6. Player-initiated *Blame Votes* (`MVOTE BLAME`) are prohibited. *Blame Votes* are used by *Referees* to issue warnings.
7. No excuses for losing will be taken into account. This includes, but is not limited to, game settings, balance-related complaints, bugs, crashes, network connection issues, drunkenness, bad luck, Moon phase, etc. Learn to lose with dignity.

## 12. Score

### 12.1. Match Score
1. Each team gets from 0 to 4 **Tourney Points** (**TP**) for each *map* (**Match**) it plays (see [TourneyPointTable]).
2. **4 TP**: the *Main Time* **win**: the opponent team was wiped out during the main 5 waves, while the team survived until the end of the wave.
3. **3 TP**: the *Overtime* **win**: the opponent team was wiped out during the *Overtime* or *Sudden Death*. The game ends one second after a team is wiped out, so the team needs to survive that second to gain 3 TP. Otherwise, both teams are wiped out, even if one survived, say, 100ms longer.
4. **2 TP**: both teams **survived**.
5. **1 TP**: both teams **wiped out** in the *Main Time*; the team survived longer (died last).
6. **1 TP**: the team was wiped out during the *Overtime* or *Sudden Death*.
7. **0 TP**: the team was wiped out during the *Main Time*.
8. The terms *Map* and *Match* are mutually substitutable in these *Tournament Rules*.
9. This rule section does not apply to *Tiebreaker*.

### 12.2. Game Outcome
1. Each game consists of two *Matches*: a *Home Map* for each team.
2. The team that earned more *Tourney Points* in the *Game* (sum of two *Matches*, **BO2**) is the **Winner**.
3. *Tourney Points* earned in previous rounds are not used to determine the *Winner* in the current *Game*.
4. If both teams **survived** both maps (`12.1.4`), the *Tiebreaker* is played to determine the *Winner*.
5. If both teams got **wiped out** in both maps (`12.1.5`), the team that survived for longer (game time sum of two maps) or, in other words, has a positive **Delta Time** (**DT**), gains an extra *TP* and is the *Winner*. For example, on the first map, Team X got wiped out at 9:00 while Team Y got wiped out at 9:15. On the second map, X survived for 6:45 while Y got wiped out at 6:00. X's total time alive is 15:45, Y's 15:15 => X is the *Winner* with DT +0:30.
6. If both teams got **wiped out** in both maps at the same time (`12.1.7`) or their *Delta Time* is 0:00 (`12.1.5`), the *Tiebreaker* is played to determine the *Winner*.
7. *Delta Time* is calculated with **one game second precision** (which may be slowed down by *Zed Time*). Surviving a few milliseconds longer doesn't count.
8. If both teams scored **4 TP** in the *Game* via the `12.1.2` or `12.1.3` outcomes, the **second** map's *Winner* gains an extra *TP* and is the *Winner* of the *Game*. Reasoning: given TSC's defensive PvEvP nature, it takes more effort to win a match from a losing position (the second map) than from equal positions (the first map).
9. Any other tie is decided by the *Tiebreaker*.
10. The *Winner* of the *Tiebreaker* gains an extra *TP* and is the *Winner* of the *Game*.
11. In extremely rare cases, the *Tiebreaker* must be replayed to determine the *Winner*, for example, if the same explosion or a *Siren* scream killed both teams.

## 13. Rank
1. Teams are ranked by the *Bracket*: `UB > LB`.
2. Teams within the same bracket are ranked by *Tourney Points*. More *TP* = higher place in the *Tournament Table*.
3. If two and **only** two teams in the same bracket have the same *TP* and they have played each other, the *Winner* of their last mutual game is ranked higher.
4. Otherwise, teams in the same bracket with the same *TP* are ranked by their *Game* Win (W) / Loss (L) history, compared from the most recent game backwards. Teams that won the last game rank higher than those that lost. If multiple teams have the same outcome in the last game, the second-to-last game is considered, and so on. For example, `LWL > WLL` (the oldest game first).
5. If multiple teams in the same bracket have the same *TP* and identical *Game* Win/Loss history, they are ranked by *Map* Win count (`12.1.2`, `12.1.3`). More *Map* Wins = higher rank.
6. If none of the above rules can determine the teams' *Rank*, they are ranked by sign-up order. Those who signed up earlier are ranked higher.

## 14. Bracket
1. The *Tournament* uses the **Double Elimination** bracket.
2. The *Winners* of the *Preliminary Round* (**W**) advance to the **Upper Bracket** (**UB**).
3. The *Losers* of the *Preliminary Round* (**L**) are moved to the **Lower Bracket** (**LB**).
4. The *Winners* of the *Upper Bracket Games* (**WW**) keep their place in the *Upper Bracket*, one eventually reaching the **Grand Finals**.
5. The *Losers* of the *Upper Bracket Games* (**WL**) are demoted to the *Lower Bracket*.
6. The *Winners* of the *Lower Bracket Games* (**LW**) keep their place in the *Lower Bracket*, one eventually reaching the **Minor Finals**.
7. The *Losers* of the *Lower Bracket Games* (**LL**) are eliminated from the *Tournament*.
8. The *Winner* of the *Minor Finals* advances to the *Grand Finals*.
9. The *Grand Finals* are played under the **Full Double Elimination** rules, meaning that **WWW** needs to be eliminated **twice**.

## 15. Tournament Schedule
1. All *Tournament Games* are scheduled according to the [Bracket].
2. In the *Preliminary Round*, team pairs are randomly selected.
3. In all other rounds, the team with the highest *Rank* in the *Bracket* is matched against the team with the lowest *Rank* in the same *Bracket*. For example, in *Week 2*, team pairs are: `W1-W4`, `W2-W3`, `L5-L8`, `L6-L7`, where `1-8` is the team *Rank* (position) in the *Tournament Table*.
4. The exception to the above rule is when a potential team pair has already met in previous rounds, and there are other pairing options within the *Bracket*. For example, the default LB team pairs in *Week 3* are `L3-L6` and `L4-L5`. However, if `L3-L6` or `L4-L5` have already met in the *Preliminary Round*, the *Week 3* pairings will be `L3-L5` and `L4-L6`.

## 17. Spectating
1. The *TA* reserves the right to broadcast/stream every match and invite special guests to spectate and commentate on a match.
2. The livestream must be delayed by **120 seconds**.
3. Players should note that their in-game actions may be revealed to the opposing team within two minutes through livestream spying. Since the *TA* has no measures to prevent this, it is not monitored.
4. *Streamers* or *Guests* **MUST NOT** be in the live voice or text chat with the players during the game.
5. Nobody on the server, including players, is allowed to post the game results on the public channels ([Discord], [SteamGroup]) during the livestream delay to avoid spoiling the outcome for live viewers. Wait for the official *TA* announcement that the game has ended before discussing it.
6. Players may livestream their own gameplay. The `17.2` requirement does not apply to self-streaming.
7. Only *Referees*, official *Streamers*, and *Guests* are allowed to spectate the game from the third-person view or freely roam the map (Free Camera).
8. *Dead* or *Reserve* players must be locked to their teammates' **first-person PoV**, unless their team got wiped out.
9. If a *Dead* or *Reserve* player doesn't get automatically locked to their teammates' first-person PoV, they must manually do it ASAP (by pressing the LMB/Fire button) and inform the *Referee* about the incident. Continuing to violate the above rule or not informing the *Referee* may be considered a **game-breaking** *Exploit*.
10. A *Reserve Player* may join the KF server as a spectator and spectate their teammates according to the `17.8` rule.
11. A *Reserve Player* may be in live chat with their team.
12. A spectating *Reserve Player* **MUST** record their in-game footage and share it with the *TA*. Failing to do so may be considered a **game-breaking** *Exploit*.
13. A *Reserve Player* may take a teammate's player seat if the latter has disconnected from the server due to technical issues.
14. If a player reconnects after the *Reserve Player* has taken their seat, the former is considered a *Reserve Player* from now on and must follow the respective rules, including the footage record requirement, or leave the server.
15. Only **one** *Reserve Player* is permitted to spectate the game on the KF server. If the team has multiple *Reserve Players* according to the `4.8` rule, they must choose one for the *Game*.

## Force Majeure
The *TA* reserves the right to make any decisions they see as right and fair in any cases that are not covered by these *Tournament Rules*.
