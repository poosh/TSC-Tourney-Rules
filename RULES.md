# TSC26 Tournament Rules

This is the official set of rules for **Team Survival Competition Tournament 2026** (**TSC2** or, in this document, Tournament).

---

## General

1. This Tournament is meant to be fun for everyone involved, so please encourage good behavior and fair play.
2. Not knowing the Tournament Rules is never a valid excuse for not following them.
3. References to any gender in any Tournament Rules do not imply these rules are gender-specific.
4. *Tournament Administration* (*TA*) reserves the right to broadcast/stream every match and invite special guests to spectate and commentate on a match.
5. *Tournament Administration* reserves the right to refuse entry to any team or player for whatever reason they see fit.
6. *Tournament Administration* reserves the right to change any rule if they find it unfair, exploitable, or/and imperfect.

### Entrance Fee

1. There is no Entrance Fee in this Tournament.
2. There is no Deposit in this Tournament.

### Prizes

1. 1st place: TSC Trophy (:crystaltrophy:) name icon and [High Decorated Player](https://steamcommunity.com/groups/ScrNBalance/discussions/7/3198115339546210521/) status. If you are already an HDP, you may gift it to a friend.
2. 2nd place: TSC Star name icon and the HDP status.
3. 3rd place: TSC Star name icon and stylized perk icon (a.k.a. prestige perks) next to their name.
4. 4th place: TSC Star name icon.

### Sign Up and Team Composition

1. Each team must consist of 4 players.
2. Each team member must have the original Killing Floor game in their Steam account.
3. Team captain must join the Steam Tournament Group. Other team members are encouraged to join it as well.
4. Additionally, a team may sign up the 5th member as a reserve player to substitute a team member if the latter cannot attend a tournament game.
5. A reserve player receives the same prizes as the other team members even if the former hasn't played a single game.
6. A team may replace any of its members *before* the Tournament has begun.
7. During the Tournament, a team may replace only the reserve player if the latter hasn't played a single game.
8. During the Tournament, a team may invite an additional reserve player if all its members (including the reserve player) have played at least one game. For example, the first game is played by team members P1, P2, P3, and P4; P4 couldn't attend the second game and got substituted by P5; the team can sign up P6 as a reserve player for the third game.
9. Players cannot switch teams.
10. The only exception to the above rule is a reserve player who hasn't played a single game and received written permission from the former team's captain to join another team.
11. All games are played in 4v4 format.
12. In an emergency case, a team may play a tournament game with 3 players (in 3v4 format) if they cannot find a 4th member.
13. In a 3v4 game, TSC automatically balances team health by granting 133hp and +33% Syringe healing rate to the smaller team members. Other than that, teams are treated equally, and being a smaller team is not an excuse for losing the game or asking for any advantage or compensation.
14. If a team cannot get even 3 players for a game, the team gets disqualified from the Tournament, loses its place in the Tournament Table, doesn't get any prizes, and loses its Deposit.
15. If a team starts a tournament game with only 3 players, all its other members who didn't participate in the Tournament get disqualified from the Tournament and don't get prizes.

### Home Maps

1. Each team captain chooses two *unique* home maps from the list below.
2. Teams cannot select maps that are already chosen by other teams, nor select the same map twice.
3. A team may choose either two Vanilla/Enhanced maps or one Vanilla/Enhanced + one Custom map. Choosing two Custom maps is prohibited.
4. The chosen home maps cannot be changed during the Tournament, unless approved or insisted upon by the *Tournament Administration*.

### Game Settings

1. Mods: the latest officially released version of ScrN Brutal KF Bundle.
2. Mutators: ScrnSpeedMut.
3. Game Type: TSCGame
4. Game Length: 42. TSC Tourney (6 waves + 1 Overtime wave + 1 Sudden Death wave)
5. Game Difficulty: Hell on Earth.
6. Perk level: 6.
7. Players spawn with no cash (£0).
8. No custom weapons and perks are allowed during the Tournament.
9. Gunslinger and Combat Medic perks are allowed due to being an official part of ScrN Balance.
10. All stock weapons (KFMod.\*) are replaced with their balanced counterparts (ScrnBalanceSrv.\*). Stock weapons without balanced counterparts are disabled.
11. Balanced (SE) weapon versions of all three Community Weapon Packs are allowed.
12. Golden, Camo, and Neon weapons are accessible via the "GunSkin" console command or "ScrN Features". Players must own the respective DLCs to unlock them.
13. DWARFS Axe and both ZED Eradication Devices (ZED Guns) are prohibited.
14. The game uses the ScrN version of vanilla zeds with fixed hitboxes and balance improvements (in the default ScrN Bundle's configuration).
15. Custom and Event zeds are prohibited.

### Tiebreaker Game Settings

1. Tiebreaker map overrides the above Game Settings with the following changes.
2. Game Length: 47. XCM Duel (0 waves + 0 Overtime waves + 10 Sudden Death waves).
3. TA announces the Tiebreaker map for each game during the game-scheduling process.

### Game Scheduling

1. *Tournament Administration* (TA) announces team pairs by posting game info on group forums and Discord.
2. Each pair consists of two teams: Team A and Team B.
3. In Preliminary Round 1, team pairs are chosen randomly.
4. Starting from the second round, teams are selected by their ranks in the Tournament Table by matching the highest with the lowest team. For example, in game 1, Team A is the tournament leader, while Team B has the lowest score among all qualified teams; in game 2, Team A is in 2nd place in the TT, while Team B has the 2nd lowest rank, etc.
5. Game server selection depends on the team regions. If teams are from different regions (e.g., NA vs. EU), then each of the two maps is set on a different server, where each team plays its home map on the opponent's server (e.g., the NA team plays its home map on the EU server while the EU team plays its home map on the NA server).
6. Mixed-region teams (that have both EU and NA members) are always playing on their opponent's server.
7. Both teams can agree to play on a specific server regardless of the region.
8. Both team captains can agree on changing the scheduled date/time if the TA confirms it.
9. Each team captain picks up one of the opponent's home maps to play.
10. Team A's home map is played first.
11. Both selected maps must be played in a row, even if they are set on different servers.
12. If the game is set on two servers, the home map must be played on the opponent's home server. Immediately after completing the first map, teams switch to another server to play the second map.
13. The Tiebreaker map is played on the same server as the last map.
14. If the team lacks members for a game, the team captain must find a substitute or agree to start a game with a smaller team. That doesn't obligate the opponent team to do the same; they can play in full strength.
15. The minimum team size is 3 players. If the team has fewer players for a game, the team is disqualified from the Tournament and loses its Deposit.

### Warmup

1. Before the match starts, teams may play a warmup wave to check if everything is fine.
2. After the warmup, the map is restarted, and the tournament game begins.
3. When the game has begun, no excuses will be taken into account (such as disconnecting, crashing, high ping, etc.). If you have crashed, then reconnect as quickly as possible. If your team got wiped because of it, that's on you.
4. TA may prohibit warmups if the game schedule is overdue.

### Perk Selection

1. Any player can select any available perk
2. There are no player-per-perk limits. For instance, the team can consist of 4 Berserkers.
3. Players may change perks every wave.

### Scoring

Each team gets from 0 to 4 **Tourney Points** per each map it plays.

- 4 points: your team survived, the opponent team got wiped during the main 6 waves.
- 3 points, your team survived, the opponent team got wiped during the Overtime or Sudden Death.
- 2 points: both teams survived.
- 1 point: your team got wiped during the Overtime or Sudden Death.
- 0 points: your team got wiped during the main waves.

### Double Elimination

1. Each game between two teams is played on two maps (one home map of each team) - BO2 (Best Of 2).
2. The team that earned more Tourney Points in the *game* (sum of two maps) is the winner.
3. The team with fewer points is the loser and gets an elimination.
4. Getting two eliminations ends the Tournament for the team (double elimination).
5. If both teams score the same number of TP in two maps, a Tiebreaker map is played to determine the winner.
6. In extremely rare cases, the Tiebreaker map must be replayed to determine the winner. For example, if the same explosion or a Siren scream killed both teams.
7. Winning a Tiebreaker grants one additional Tourney Point.
8. Tourney Points earned in previous rounds are not used to determine the winner in the current game.

### Preliminary Round (PR)

1. In the Preliminary Round, team pairs are randomly selected.
2. The winners (W) of the preliminary games advance to the Upper Bracket.
3. The losers (L) of the preliminary games are moved to the Lower Bracket.

### Upper Bracket (UB)

1. The winner advances to the next round, up to the Grand Final.
2. The loser drops down to the Lower Bracket.

### Lower Bracket (UB)

1. The winner advances to the next round, up to Minor Final.
2. The loser gets eliminated from the Tournament.

### Minor Final

1. The Minor Final is played between the winner of the Lower Bracket and the loser of the Upper Bracket (WL).
2. The winner advances to the Grand Final.
3. The loser ends the Tournament in the 3rd place.

### Grand Final

1. The Grand Final is played between the winner of the Upper Bracket and the winner of the Minor Final.
2. Unlike the previous rounds, the Grand Final is played in a single-elimination playoff format. The Upper Bracket winner needs only one elimination to lose, and the prior loss of the Minor Final's winner is ignored.
3. Nonetheless, the UB winner receives a 0.5 Tourney Point advantage and the right to choose the map order as an award for not having a single loss in the Tournament.
4. There is no Tiebreaker in the Grand Final, as both teams cannot score the same TP due to a 0.5 TP advantage of the UB winner.
5. Grand Final uses BO4 to determine the winner by playing both home maps of both teams (4 maps in total).
6. Grand Final may end prematurely if one of the teams loses a theoretical chance to win (more than 8 TP advantage after 2 maps or more than 4 TP advantage after 3 maps).
7. The winner of the Grand Final wins the Tournament.
8. The loser of the Grand Final ends the Tournament in 2nd place.

### Final Standings

- 1st place: The winner of the Grand Final.
- 2nd place: The loser of the Grand Final.
- 3rd place: The loser of the Minor Final.
- Other places are determined by their rank in the Tournament Table.

### Ranking

1. Teams are ranked by Tourney Points (TP). More TP = higher place in the Tournament Table.
2. If two and only two teams have the same TP and they have played with each other, the winner of the last mutual game is ranked higher.
3. If multiple teams have the same TP, they are ranked by their game win count (more wins = higher rank).
4. If multiple teams have the same TP and win count, they are ranked by their game/map Wins/Loses in the reverse order. Those who won the last game are ranked higher than those who lost. If the last game has the same outcome for multiple teams, the second last is considered, and so on. For example, LWL > WLL.
5. If multiple teams have the same TP and identical win/loss history, they are ranked by sign-up order. Those who signed up earlier are ranked higher.

### In-game Voting (MVOTE)

1. Players are allowed to initiate `MVOTE BORING` if they feel the game pace is too slow.
2. If `MVOTE BORING` failed, the initiator team is prohibited from repeating the vote until the end of the wave.
3. Dead players are not allowed to call `MVOTE BORING`.
4. Each team captain has the right to initiate a 1-minute pause (MVOTE PAUSE 60) twice in the game (once per map; twice on the same map and none on another; or even twice in a row) or ask a referee to force it at the end of the current wave.
5. Referees have a right to do technical pauses for various reasons, including player crashes/disconnects, but they are not obligated to do so.
6. Pauses can be called only at the start of Trader Time (during the first 20 seconds).
7. Pauses during a wave are prohibited, except for player crashes/disconnects.
8. Pauses during the Tiebreaker are prohibited.
9. If a team encounters technical issues at the map start (player crash, disconnect, visual glitches, etc.), the team captain can ask for a map restart (`MVOTE MAP RESTART`).
10. Map restart must be initiated within the first 30 gameplay seconds of the match.
11. The team that initiated a map restart loses its pause-voting right.
12. A team cannot initiate a map restart if it used all its pause votes.
13. Map restarts during the Tiebreaker are prohibited.

### Exploits, Glitches and Tricks

1. Using any exploit in the game leads to a warning (MVOTE BLAME).
2. If the player continues using an exploit after a warning, they get forcibly killed by a referee. However, they can return to the game in the next wave.
3. If the previous rule wasn't enough and the player continues using the exploit after returning to the game, then they get kicked and banned from the game. Additionally, the TA may decide to disqualify this player or the entire team from the Tournament.
4. If a use of an exploit has game-breaking consequences, a referee or the *Tournament Administration* has the right to make the following decisions:
   - a) stop the game, giving both teams points for waves survived before the issue happened;
   - b) force the game's replay;
   - c) disqualify the exploiter team from the Tournament;
   - d) revoke the Deposit.
5. Player suicides are legal.
6. Weapon tricks and combos are not considered exploits and are legal.
7. Players are not allowed to stay in place unreachable by zeds. If a player stays in such a place, the referee issues a warning.
8. Player has 30 seconds to leave the "glitch spot" after receiving a warning, or it will be considered an exploit with all the consequences.
9. Returning to the same "glitch spot" by the player or their teammates is considered an exploit, and the player gets forcibly killed by the referee without a warning.
10. If a team collects 15+ zeds behind a welded door, the referee informs the opponent team about the case, revealing the location of the door.
11. If the door is under zed attack, then its continuous welding is allowed only if the team is scoring at least 20 zed kills per minute.
12. Total welding inside the room(-s) or area(-s), where all doors are welded, and zeds are unable to reach the team, is considered an exploit. Having a few zed spawns inside the welded area isn't an excuse. This rule doesn't apply during the Trader Time or when an opponent player is inside the welded area.
13. Switching to spectator mode during a game is considered an exploit and may have game-breaking consequences.
14. Using third-party tools ("hacks") that give players an unfair advantage (aimbots, wallhacks, crosshairs, etc.) during the game is strictly prohibited and leads to disqualification.
15. Every player is encouraged to record their entire tournament gameplay footage.

### Refereeing and Player Conduct

1. All decisions made by referees during a game are final and unchangeable, unless the game is under investigation by the *Tournament Administration*.
2. If the problem is too complex and cannot be solved by a referee during the game, it will be investigated by the *Tournament Administration* (TA) after the match. The referee informs teams and viewers when such a problem occurs.
3. Any complaints regarding a tournament game must be posted on the Game Page during the game or within 30 minutes after its end. If no complaints are received during that time, the game is officially closed, and no further investigation can be done.
4. Human factor must be taken into account in refereeing. Referees may forget to force "MVOTE PAUSE" or similar. Referees may not detect exploits or other rule violations. Players or livestream viewers are advised to inform referees or the TA as soon as possible when such cases occur. That can be done by posting on the Game Page, in the Discord, and/or in the livestream chat.
5. Personal attacks, insults, antagonism of any Tournament members, referees, or administrators via in-game chat, Tournament Group discussions, or comments will not be tolerated and will lead to disqualification.
6. Blame-votes (MVOTE BLAME) are allowed within the own team only. Players are not allowed to blame opponents, referees, *Tournament Administration*, zeds, or Baron.
7. No excuses for losing will be taken into account. That includes, but is not limited to, game settings, balance-related complaints, bugs, crashes, network connection, drunkenness, bad luck, Moon phase, etc. Learn to lose with dignity.

### Force Majeure

1. *Tournament Administration* reserves the right to make any decisions they see as right and fair in any cases that are not covered by these rules.
