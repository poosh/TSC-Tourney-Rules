## 1. General
1. This *Tournament* is meant to be fun for everyone involved, so please encourage good behavior and fair play.

3. References to any gender in any *Tournament Rules* do not imply these rules are gender-specific.
4. *TA* reserves the right to refuse entry to any team or player for whatever reason they see fit.
5. *TA* reserves the right to change any rule if they find it unfair, exploitable, or/and imperfect.

## 4. Sign Up and Team Composition
1. Each team must consist of 4 players.
2. Each team member must have the original **Killing Floor** game in their *Steam* account.
3. Each team's **Captain** must join the [SteamGroup] and [Discord]. Other team members are encouraged to join as well.
4. Additionally, a team may sign up the 5th member as a **Reserve Player** to substitute for a team member if the latter cannot attend a tournament game.
5. A *Reserve Player* receives the same prizes as the other team members even if the former hasn't played a single game.
6. A team may replace any of its members **before** the *Tournament* has begun.
7. During the *Tournament*, a team may replace only the *Reserve Player* if the latter hasn't played a single game.
8. During the *Tournament*, a team may invite an additional reserve player if all its members (including the *Reserve Player*) have played at least one game. For example, team members P1, P2, P3, and P4 play the first game; P4 can't attend the second game and is substituted by P5; the team can sign up P6 as a *Reserve Player* for the third game. The rule `#.5` applies to both reserve players in this case.
9. Players cannot switch teams.
10. The only exception to the above rule is a *Reserve Player* who hasn't played a single game and received written permission from the former team's captain to join another team.
11. All games are played in **4v4** format.

## 9. Game Schedule

### 9.1. Team Pair Selection
1. *Tournament Administration* announces team pairs by posting game info on [SteamGroup] forums and [Discord].
2. Each pair consists of two teams: **Team A** and **Team B**, where *Team A* is the team with higher *Rank* in the *Tournament table*.
3. In the *Preliminary Round*, team pairs are chosen randomly and treated equally. There is no *Team A/B* in this round.
4. Both team *Captains* can agree on changing the scheduled date/time if the *Tournament Administration* approves it.

### 9.2. Game Server Selection
1. If both teams are from the same region, they play on their region's server. **SA** teams play on the **NA** server.
2. In a **NA vs. EU** game, each of the two maps is set on a different server, where a team plays its *Home Map* on the opponent's *Home Server*: *NA Home Map* is played on *EU Server* while *EU Home Map* is played on *NA Server*.
3. **NA vs. SA** games are played on the **NA** server.
4. **SA vs. EU** games get special treatment, as most SA-EU traffic goes via the USA's East Coast, making the **NA** server the perfect middle ground (compromise) for these games, where all players have **~100** *PING*.
5. The first map of a **SA vs. EU** game is always played on the **NA** server.
6. If 3 members of the **SA** team had an average *PING* above **80** and the 4th member had it above **50**, the second game is also played on the **NA** server. Otherwise, the **SA** team is treated as an **NA** team, and the second map is played on the **EU** server according to the rule `#.2`.
7. The rule `#.5` applies only if the the *EU Home Map* was played first.
8. The *Tiebreaker* map is played on the same server as the last map.
9. **Mixed**-region teams are always playing on their opponent's server.
10. Both teams can agree to play on a specific server regardless of the region.

### 9.3. Home Map Selection
1. The team *Captain* chooses one of the opponent's *Home Maps* to play.
2. *Team A's Captain* chooses the map play order, except for the *Preliminary Round*, where the teams are chosen randomly.
3. *Tournament Administration* announces the **deadline** for the map/order selection.
4. If the team *Captain* hasn't posted their map choice before the *deadline*, the least recently played *Home Map* gets automatically selected (or the `Home Map #1` if that is the first team's game in the Tournament).
5. If the *Team A's Captain* hasn't posted their map order choice before the *deadline*, the *Team A's Home Map* is played first, except for **SA vs. EU**, where the *EU Home Map on the NA Server* is played first to match the `9.2.4 - 9.2.7` rules.
6. The **Game #12** forces the rule `#.4`, as the *deadline* expires before the team pair is known.

## 10. Game Progress
### 10.1. Warmup
1. Both teams should join the server **10 minutes** before their game schedule.
2. **5 minutes** before the schedule, the *Warmup* match begins to check if everything is fine (*PING*, config, etc.).
3. If both teams lack players 5 minutes before the scheduled time, *Warmup* gets skipped.
4. *Warmup* match begins regardless of the other team's player count; even "4 vs. 0" is acceptable. Late joiners miss the *Warmup*.
5. *Warmup* is played on the same map as the upcoming *Match*.
6. The *Warmup* consists of *Wave 1* and the following *Trader Time*.
7. At the start of *Wave 2*, the map gets restarted (`MVOTE MAP RESTART`) and the *Tournament Game* begins.
8. Both teams can agree to skip the *Warmup*.
9. *Warmup* stats (kills, deaths, etc.) are excluded from *Tournament Statistics* and have no effect on the *Tournament Game*.

### 10.2. Game Start
1. The *Tournament Game* starts **exactly** at the scheduled time unless delayed by the *Tournament Administration*. For example, if the previous game is overdue.
2. The team *Captain* has the right to delay the start of the game for up to **10 minutes** due to technical difficulties. In that case, the team loses its pause-voting (`10.5`) and map-restarting (`10.6`) rights.
3. In an emergency case, a team may start a game with **3 players** (in **3v4** format) if they cannot find a 4th member.
4. In a **3v4** game, TSC automatically balances team health by granting **133hp** and **+33% Syringe** healing rate to the smaller team members. Otherwise, teams are treated equally, and being the smaller team doesn't excuse losing the game or asking for any advantage or compensation.
5. The 4th player may join in later waves if they couldn't make it for the start.
6. If a team doesn't have even **3 players** for the game start, it gets **disqualified** from the *Tournament*, loses its place in the *Tournament Table*, doesn't get any *Prizes*, and loses its *Deposit*.
7. If a team starts a game with only **3 players**, all its other members who didn't participate in the *Tournament Games* get **disqualified** from the *Tournament* and don't get *Prizes*. The `4.5` rule no longer applies to the team.

### 10.3. Second Map
1. The second map is played in **Short** mode (5 *Main Waves*, no *Overtime*) if one of the team scored 4 *Tourney Points* in the first map (`4:0`, *Main Time* win).
2. The second map is scheduled in **5 minutes** after the first one ends if both are played on the same server.
3. The second map is scheduled **10 minutes** after the first one ends if it needs to be played on a different server.
4. The second *Warmup* is played only if maps are played on different servers.
5. A team cannot initiate the map restart (`10.6`) if it triggered the map restart or used **all** its pause votes in the previous map.

### 10.4. Tiebreaker
1. *Tiebreaker*, if needed, is scheduled in **5 minutes** after the second map and is played on the same server as the second map.
2. The *Second Map* rules (`10.3`) also apply to *Tiebreaker*.
3. *Game Pause* (`10.5`) and *Map Restart* (`10.6`) are prohibited in *Tiebreaker*.

### 10.5. Game Pause
1. Each team *Captain* has the right to initiate a **1-minute pause** (`MVOTE PAUSE 60`) **twice** in the game (once per map; twice on the same map and none on another; or even twice in a row) or ask a *Referee* to force it at the end of the current wave.
2. Pauses can be called only at the start of *Trader Time*, during its first 20 seconds.
3. Pauses during a wave are prohibited.

### 10.6. Map Restart
1. If a team encounters technical issues at the map start (player crash, disconnect, visual glitches, etc.), the *Captain* may ask for a **map restart** (`MVOTE MAP RESTART` or ask the *Referee* to do so).
2. Map restart must be initiated within the first **30 gameplay seconds** of the match. The *Captain* can quickly chat "re" first (to meet the 30-second mark), then explain the case in the follow-up message.
3. The team that initiated a map restart loses its pause-voting rights (`10.5`).
4. After the first **30 gameplay seconds** of the match, no excuses are taken into account (such as disconnecting, crashing, high ping, etc.). If you crash, reconnect as quickly as possible. If your team got wiped because of it, that's on you.

### 10.7. Technical Issues
1. Crashed/disconnected players may rejoin the game mid-wave, unless their team got wiped out during the reconnect.
2. Mid-wave rejoins are prohibited during a *Sudden Death* wave.
3. To keep a fair attitude but prevent cheating in a *Sudden Death* wave, only **healthy** players (`80+ HP`) do not cause their team to wipe on a crash/disconnect.

### 10.8. Boring Vote
1. Players are allowed to initiate a *Boring Vote* (`MVOTE BORING`) to boost the zed spawn rate if they feel the game pace is too slow.
2. If the *Boring Vote* fails, the initiator team is prohibited from repeating the vote until the end of the wave.
3. Dead players are not allowed to initiate a *Boring Vote*.
4. A *Boring Vote* is prohibited in a **3v4** game.

### 10.9. Perk Selection
1. Any player can select any available perk
2. There are no player-per-perk limits. For instance, the team can consist of 4 Berserkers.
3. Players may change perks every wave.

### 10.10. Weapon Combos
1. **Weapon Combos** that allow partially skipping the weapon fire or reload animation by switching to another weapon are **legal**.
2. **Syringe** instant self-heal alias is **legal**.
3. Any commands, aliases, or macros that allow the player to bypass the weapon's fire rate and achieve DPS higher than intended are considered a **game-breaking exploit**.

### 10.11. Door Welding
1. TSC automatically detects **10+ zeds behind a welded door** and **marks** the door for all players.
2. The *marked* door gets auto-destroyed in **20 seconds** (**10s** in *Overtime*) if it is still under the zed attack.
3. The door mark gets reset in 30s after the last zed attack.
4. **Total welding** inside a room or an area, where **all** doors are welded, and zeds are unable to reach the team, is considered an **exploit** unless there are two or more active hidden zed spawn volumes (`ZombieVolume`) that cannot be blocked by player proximity or visibility.
5. The above rule is not applied during *Trader Time* or when an opponent player is inside the welded area.

### 10.12. Exploits, Glitches and Tricks
1. Using any **exploit** in the game leads to a warning (`MVOTE BLAME`).
2. If the player continues using the *Exploit* after the warning, they get forcibly killed by a *Referee*. However, they can return to the game in the next wave.
3. If the previous rule wasn't enough and the player continues using the *Exploit* after returning to the game, then they get kicked and banned from the game. Additionally, the *TA* may decide to **disqualify** this player or the entire team from the *Tournament*.
4. If a use of an *Exploit* leads to **game-breaking** consequences, a *Referee* or the *Tournament Administration* has the right to make the following decisions:
   - a) **stop** the game, giving both teams points for waves survived before the issue happened;
   - b) force the game's **replay**;
   - c) **disqualify** the exploiter or the entire team from the *Tournament*;
   - d) revoke the *Deposit*.
5. Player `SUICIDE` is legal.
6. Players are not allowed to stay in a place **unreachable** by zeds (**Glitch Spot**). If a player stays there, it is considered an **exploit**.
7. Switching to **spectator** mode during a game is considered an *Exploit* and may have game-breaking consequences.
8. Using **third-party tools** (**hacks**) that give players an unfair advantage (aimbots, wallhacks, crosshairs, etc.) during the game is strictly prohibited and leads to **disqualification**.
9. Every player is encouraged to record their entire *Tournament* gameplay to prove their gameplay is legit.

## 11. Refereeing and Player Conduct
1. All decisions made by *Referees* during a game are final and unchangeable, unless the game is under investigation by the *Tournament Administration*.
2. If the problem is too complex and cannot be solved by a *Referee* during the game, it will be investigated by the *TA* after the match. The *Referee* informs teams and viewers when such a problem occurs.
3. Any complaints regarding a *Tournament Game* must be posted on the [SteamGroup] or [Discord] during the game or within **1 hour** after its end. If no complaints are received within that time, the game is officially closed, and no further investigation will be conducted.
4. Human factor must be taken into account in refereeing. *Referees* may forget to force `MVOTE PAUSE` or similar. *Referees* may not detect *Exploits* or other rule violations. Players or livestream viewers are advised to inform *Referees* or the *TA* as soon as possible when such cases occur. That can be done by posting on the [SteamGroup], in the [Discord], and/or in the official *Livestream Chat*.
5. Personal attacks, insults, antagonism of any *Tournament* members, *Referees*, or *TA* via in-game chat, [SteamGroup] discussions, or comments will not be tolerated and will lead to **disqualification**.
6. Blame-votes (`MVOTE BLAME`) are allowed within the own team only. Players may not blame opponents, *Referees*, *Tournament Administration*, zeds, or *Baron*.
7. No excuses for losing will be taken into account. This includes, but is not limited to, game settings, balance-related complaints, bugs, crashes, network connection issues, drunkenness, bad luck, Moon phase, etc. Learn to lose with dignity.

## 12. Score
### 12.1. Match Score
1. Each team gets from 0 to 4 **Tourney Points** (**TP**) per each *map* (**Match**) it plays (see [TourneyPointTable]):
2. **4 TP**: The *Main Time* **win**: the opponent team was wiped out during the main 5 waves, while the team survived until the end of the wave.
3. **3 TP**: The *Overtime* **win**: the opponent team was wiped out during the *Overtime* or *Sudden Death*. The game ends one second after a team is wiped out, so the team needs to survive that second to gain 3 TP. Otherwise, both teams are wiped out, even if one survived, say, 100ms longer.
4. **2 TP**: both teams **survived**.
5. **1 TP**: both teams **wiped out** in the *Main Time*; the team survived longer (died last).
6. **1 TP**: The *Overtime* **loss**: the team was wiped out during the *Overtime* or *Sudden Death*.
7. **0 TP**: The *Main Time* **loss**: the team was wiped out during the *Main Time*.
8. The terms *Map* and *Match* are mutually substitutable in these *Rules*.
9. This rule section does not apply to *Tiebreaker*.

### 12.2. Game Outcome
1. Each game consists of two *Matches*: a *Home Map* for each team.
2. The team that earned more *Tourney Points* in the *Game* (sum of two *Matches*, **BO2**) is the **Winner**.
3. *Tourney Points* earned in previous rounds are not used to determine the **Winner** in the current *Game*.
4. If both teams **survived** both maps (`12.1.4`), the *Tiebreaker* is played to determine the *Winner*.
5. If both teams got **wiped out** in both maps (`12.1.5`), the team that survived for longer (game time sum of two maps) or, in other words, has the "positive game time delta", gains an extra **TP** and is the **Winner**. For example, on the first map, Team A got wiped out at 9:00 while B got wiped out at 9:15. On the second map, A survived for 6:45 while B got wiped out at 6:00. A's total time alive is 15:45, B's 15:15 => A is the winner with DT+0:30.
6. If both teams got **wiped out** in both maps at the same time (`12.1.7`) or their delta time is 0:00 (`12.1.5`), the *Tiebreaker* is played to determine the *Winner*.
7. *Delta Time* is calculated with **one game second precision** (which may be slowed down by *Zed Time*). Surviving a few milliseconds longer doesn't count.
8. If both teams scored **4 TP** in the *Game* via the `12.1.2` or `12.1.3` outcomes, the **second** map's *Winner* gains an extra **TP** and is the **Winner** of the *Game*. Reasoning: given TSC's defensive PvEvP nature, it takes more effort to win a match from a losing position (the second map) than from equal positions (the first map).
9. The *Winner* of the *Tiebreaker* gains an extra **TP** and is the **Winner** of the *Game*.
10. In extremely rare cases, the *Tiebreaker* must be replayed to determine the *Winner*. For example, if the same explosion or a *Siren* scream killed both teams.

## 13. Rank
1. Teams are ranked by the *Bracket*: `UB > LB`.
2. Teams within the same bracket are ranked by *Tourney Points*. More *TP* = higher place in the *Tournament Table*.
3. If two and **only** two teams in the same bracket have the same *TP* and they have played each other, the *Winner* of the last mutual game is ranked higher.
4. If multiple teams in the same bracket have the same *TP*, they are ranked by their *Game Win* count (more wins = higher rank).
5. If multiple teams in the same bracket have the same *TP* and *Game Win* count, they are ranked by their Game/Match Wins (W) / Losses (L) in reverse order. Teams that won the last game rank higher than those that lost. If multiple teams have the same outcome in the last game, the second-to-last game is considered, and so on. For example, `LWL > WLL`.
6. If multiple teams in the same bracket have the same *TP* and identical Win/Loss history, they are ranked by sign-up order. Those who signed up earlier are ranked higher.

## 14. Bracket
1. The *Tournament* uses the **Double Elimination** bracket.
2. The *Winners* of the *Preliminary Round* (**W**) advance to the **Upper Bracket** (**UB**).
3. The *Losers* of the *Preliminary Round* (**L**) are moved to the **Lower Bracket** (**LB**).
4. The *Winners* of the *Upper Bracket Games* (**WW**) keep their place in the **Upper Bracket**, one eventually reaching the **Grand Finals**.
5. The *Losers* of the *Upper Bracket Games* (**WL**) are demoted to the **Lower Bracket**.
6. The *Winners* of the *Lower Bracket Games* (**LW**) keep their place in the **Lower Bracket**, one eventually reaching the **Minor Finals**.
7. The *Losers* of the *Lower Bracket Games* (**LL**) are eliminated from the *Tournament*.
8. The *Winner* of the *Minor Finals* advances to the **Grand Finals**.
9. The **Grand Finals** are played under the **Full Double Elimination** rules, meaning that **WWW** needs to be eliminated **twice**.

## 15. Tournament Schedule
1. All *Tournament Games* are scheduled according to the [Bracket]
2. In the **Preliminary Round**, team pairs are randomly selected.
3. In all other rounds, the team with the highest *Rank* in the *Bracket* is matched against the team with the lowest *Rank* in the same *Bracket*. For example, in *Week 2*, team pairs are: `W1-W4`, `W2-W3`, `L5-L8`, `L6-L7`, where `1-8` is the team *Rank* (position) in the *Tournament Table*.
4. The exception to the above rule is when a potential team pair has already met in previous rounds, and there are other pairing options within the *Bracket*. For example, the default LB team pairs in *Week 3* are `L3-L6` and `L4-L5`. However, if `L3-L6` or `L4-L5` have already met in the *Preliminary Round*, the *Week 3* pairings will be `L3-L5` and `L4-L6`.

### 17. Spectating
1. **Tournament Administration** (**TA**) reserves the right to broadcast/stream every match and invite special guests to spectate and commentate on a match.
2. Livestream must be delayed for **120 seconds**.
3. Players should note that their in-game actions may be revealed to the opposing team within two minutes through livestream spying. Since **TA** has no measures to prevent this, we don't monitor it.
4. *Streamers* or *Guests* **MUST NOT** be in the live voice or text chat with the players during the game.
5. Nobody on the server, including players, is allowed to post the game results on the public channels ([Discord], [SteamGroup]) during the livestream delay to avoid spoiling the outcome for live viewers. Wait for the official **TA** announcement that the game has ended before discussing it.
6. Players may livestream their own gameplay.
7. Only *Referees*, official *Streamers*, and *Guests* are allowed to spectate the game from the 3rd-person view perspective or freely roam the map (Free Camera).
8. *Dead* or *Reserve* players must be locked to their teammates' **first-person PoV**, unless their team got wiped out.
9. If a *Dead* or *Reserve* player doesn't get automatically locked to their teammates' first-person PoV, they must manually do it ASAP (by pressing the LMB/Fire button) and inform the *Referee* about the incident. Continuing to violate the above rule or not informing the *Referee* may be considered a **game-breaking exploit**.
10. A *Reserve Player* may join the KF server as a spectator and spectate their teammates according to the `#.8` rule.
11. A *Reserve Player* may be in live chat with their team.
12. A spectating *Reserve Player* **MUST** record their in-game footage and share it with **TA**. Failing to do so may be considered a **game-breaking exploit**.
13. A *Reserve Player* may take a teammate's player seat if the latter has disconnected from the server due to technical issues.
14. If a player reconnects after the *Reserve Player* has taken their seat, the former is considered a *Reserve Player* from now on and must follow the respective rules, including the footage record requirement, or leave the server.
15. Only **one** *Reserve Player* is permitted to spectate the game on the KF server. If the team has multiple *Reserve Players* according to the `4.8` rule, they must choose one for the *Game*.

### Force Majeure
*Tournament Administration* reserves the right to make any decisions they see as right and fair in any cases that are not covered by these *Rules*.
